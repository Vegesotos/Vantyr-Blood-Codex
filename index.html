Contract of the Future Devil

A devil inhabits part of Vantyr's mind, seeing significant futures and bending their outcome. Intelligence 16+ prerequisite. Binding contract, removable only by divine intervention, Wish, or a party's death.

Absolute Foresight

Cannot be surprised while conscious. Intelligence modifier and Advantage on Initiative are already included in Stand by the Emperor; initiative stays +25.

One Step Ahead

Gains a d6 at combat start and each Vantyr turn, saving unused dice. Spend d6 to add or subtract from attack rolls, ability checks, saving throws or a creature's AC until end of turn.

Future Is Set

Once per Long Rest when rolling Initiative: Advantage on attacks, ability checks and saves; incoming attacks against you have disadvantage. Once per round when an enemy misses, move your speed without provoking opportunity attacks. Ends if incapacitated.

Foreseen Outcome

Reaction when a creature within 60 ft attacks, checks or saves: Absolute Failure (3/LR, 10 HP), Absolute Success (3/LR, 10 HP, not a crit), or Weighted Probability (unlimited, 30 HP, impose Advantage or Disadvantage). All costs include the Price of Knowing once, bypass Temp HP and resistance. Dropping to 0 from these costs leaves you at 1 HP and +1 Exhaustion.

🦇 THE ONE TRUE VAMPIRERacial powers · roleplay lore · restrictions · unlocks

Cursed Traits of the Emperor

Revulsion to the Sacred: Vantyr instinctively recoils from radiant magic and holy places. He cannot activate Emperor's Time unless he has been invited into the place or dwelling he occupies (your DM's racial restriction).

Loathing the Light: movement speed −10 ft in bright light; +10 ft in dim light or darkness.

Dreamless Hunger: can consume or draw strength from dreams. No numerical benefit was specified on the character sheet.

Stake to the Heart

A piercing weapon driven into Vantyr's heart (DM's called-shot discretion) paralyzes him until the end of his next turn.

Crimson Majesty

Innate magical influence. Suggestion once per long rest; at level 14+, Mass Suggestion instead. Uses Intelligence and Vantyr's form-based spell DC (20 / 23). Track/cast both in Spells; one shared racial use.

Blood Echo Memory

After damaging a creature with Vampiric Bite, Vantyr can cast Detect Thoughts once per long rest without components, using Intelligence. If a creature dies within one minute of the bite, Vantyr may glimpse a fleeting memory (emotion, secret, location), chosen by the DM.

Mistform Rebirth

Once per long rest, when reduced to 0 HP, Vantyr can instead drop to 1 HP and turn to mist until the end of his next turn. A second fall to 0 HP on the same day draws his body toward his coffin.

While mist: incorporeal; resistance to damage except radiant and force; move through creatures and objects as difficult terrain; heavily obscured, attacks against Vantyr have disadvantage; cannot speak, attack or cast spells; move up to 60 ft; reform in an unoccupied space within 30 ft at the end of the next turn. See your DM for timing.

Blood Sustenance — Enhanced

Vampiric Charm: after feeding with Vampiric Bite, attempt Charm Person or Charm Monster (shared 1/long rest), DC 8 + INT modifier + proficiency = 20 Normal / 23 Emperor's Time.

Bloodhungry Bite: against a stunned, grappled, restrained or other DM-approved impaired target, siphon HP up to Vantyr's missing HP (or the target's remaining HP). Siphoned HP restores Vantyr and inflicts the same amount as flat necrotic damage. The target can recover siphoned HP only through Hit Dice or a long rest.
